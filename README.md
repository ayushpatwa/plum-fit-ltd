# Plum Fit Ltd - Plumbing & Heating Services London

A modern, high-converting website built for **Plum Fit Ltd**, combining the best elements of leading London plumbing & heating contractors:
- 🚨 **24/7 Emergency Dispatch:** Prominent emergency banners, click-to-call direct dials, and 30-60 min arrival estimates across London.
- 🛡️ **Gas Safe & Manufacturer Trust Badges:** Gas Safe Register (#633735), Worcester Bosch Accredited, Vaillant Advance, and Baxi approved.
- 📋 **Transparent Pricing Schedule:** Published standard diagnostic fees, multi-tier boiler servicing (Safety Check, Safety Plus, Strip-Down), and common repairs price guide.
- 📍 **Local London Coverage & Postcode Checker:** Covers SW, SE, W, NW postcodes with interactive live coverage check.
- ⭐ **Social Proof & 6-Point Promise:** Real customer reviews, 12-month workmanship guarantees, and clean home protocols.
