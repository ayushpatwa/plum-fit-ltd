Got Questions?

Frequently Asked Questions

Clear answers about our callouts, emergency arrival times, pricing, and guarantees.

Do you charge a call-out fee or diagnostic charge? +

We do not have hidden call-out fees. For fault-finding and repairs, we charge a transparent £75 + VAT standard diagnostic visit fee. During this visit, our Gas Safe engineer physically pinpoints the exact breakdown, explains the options, and provides a fixed written price before carrying out any repair work.

How quickly can an engineer arrive in an emergency? +

Our average emergency response time across London is between 30 and 60 minutes, depending on traffic and location. For urgent hazards (such as uncontrolled water leaks or smelling gas), we dispatch our nearest available on-duty van immediately.

Are all your engineers Gas Safe registered? +

Yes. Every heating and gas engineer working for Plum Fit Ltd is registered with the Gas Safe Register (Registration #633735). You can check their official Gas Safe ID card upon arrival at your property.

What warranties do you provide on plumbing and heating jobs? +

All repair work is protected by our 12-Month Workmanship Guarantee. Replacement parts carry full manufacturer warranties. On new boiler installations from Worcester Bosch or Vaillant, you receive between 7 and 12 years of manufacturer warranty.

What payment methods do you accept? +

We accept all major debit and credit cards (Visa, Mastercard, American Express), contactless mobile payments (Apple Pay, Google Pay), and direct bank transfers. We provide instant digital VAT receipts and Gas Safe documentation via email.

Need Urgent Plumbing or Heating Assistance?

Do not let a burst pipe or boiler breakdown damage your home. Our London emergency team is ready to assist 24 hours a day, 7 days a week.
